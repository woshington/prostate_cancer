\abstract[Abstract]{Automated ISUP grade classification in prostate biopsy whole-slide images remains challenging due to high morphological variability, label noise, and inter-institutional heterogeneity. This study proposes an efficient pipeline combining entropy-based noise filtering, systematic patch extraction, ordinal regression, and focal loss to enhance robustness in learning from large-scale data. Variants of the EfficientNet architecture (B0, B3, and B7) were trained using a unified strategy, and their outputs were aggregated through ensemble methods. Experiments were conducted on the multicentric PANDA dataset, comprising 10,616 WSIs. The best individual model (EfficientNet-B0 with ordinal focal loss) achieved a QWK of 0.856, while deeper variants did not yield further improvements. The simple-average ensemble surpassed all single models, reaching a QWK of 0.879 and producing the most clinically coherent confusion matrix, with misclassifications largely restricted to adjacent ISUP grades. These results demonstrate that combining ordinal modeling, noise filtering, and lightweight architectures provides an advantageous balance between accuracy, stability, and computational efficiency. Although competitive with state-of-the-art approaches, the method requires validation on external datasets to further assess generalizability. Future directions include specialized models for intermediate grades and the exploration of transformer-based architectures.}
